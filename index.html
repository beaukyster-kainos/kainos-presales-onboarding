How we talk about ourselves

Kainos voice is honest, humble, familial, ambitious, confident. We say "partnership" not "engagement", "accelerator" not "tool". We avoid synergy, leverage-as-a-verb, best-in-class, world-class. We reference clients by name. Active voice — "Kainos delivers", not "is delivered by Kainos".

Two rules you cannot break

Pricing Guardrail

Investment, not cost

No hourly rates, no role-by-role hour breakdowns, no DB field names in any client-facing document. Phase totals must sum to overall. Round client-facing totals to the nearest $5,000.

Client Data

Never cross-pollinate

One client's data never appears in another client's deliverable. Comparable deals are anonymised — "a similar healthcare implementation", never the name. Case studies are the only pre-approved public references.

B.Your First 30 Days

A suggested path. Adjust with your manager — but in this order you'll build context before you're asked to produce anything client-facing.

  1. Week 1 — Meet the team. Read the People tab. Get a 30-minute intro with each person you'll work with regularly (your Deployment Strategy peers, your AE counterparts, the vertical leads for the industries you'll cover).
  2. Week 1 — Read the sales process. Walk through the Sales Cycle tab end-to-end. Understand the eight stages, the five gates, and which path (A, B, or C) most deals follow.
  3. Week 1 — Read the methodology. Open the SharePoint folder 01_Methodology and Playbooks. Read the Launch methodology language doc and skim the six Non-Standard Implementation components.
  4. Week 2 — Shadow two discovery calls. Ask which active pursuits are at Stage 3 (Scoping) and listen in. Read the Discovery Questionnaire (03_Estimation/1-Discovery) before each.
  5. Week 2 — Walk the estimation models. Open the Workday Functional Uplifts Catalog and the Integrations Catalog. Understand the inputs that drive a ROM and the standard cost model.
  6. Week 3 — Read three case studies. Pick three from 04_RFP/Case Studies that match the verticals you'll cover. Notice how outcomes are written.
  7. Week 3 — Read the SOPs. 05_Enablement & Training has the AI-integrated and traditional Pre-Sales SOPs plus three deep-dives (ROM, Integration Scoping, Scope Doc Generation).
  8. Week 3 — Sit in on a deal review. Watch a Stage 5 review run. Reference the Qualification Framework in 08_Sales Process and SOPs beforehand.
  9. Week 4 — Run a ROM yourself. Pick a closed-lost or sandbox deal with your manager and try the estimation workflow end-to-end. Get it reviewed before assuming it's right.

01.The Shape of a Deal

Americas runs an eight-stage sales process with five qualification gates. Deployment Strategy is the consistent throughline — SEs lead Stages 2 through 6 and own the handoff at Stage 8.

Why we have gates. Three problems the process is designed to fix: unqualified deals consuming SE bandwidth, geographic margin gaming on estimation (up to 47% cost variance historically), and scope context lost between sales and delivery. Each gate exists to close one of those leaks.

02.The Eight Stages

Stage 1Opportunity Identification

Owner: Sales (AE) · Deployment Strategy is Informed

AE captures the opportunity in Dynamics CRM. Minimum data: client name, industry, EE count, budget status, timeline, Workday AE/RSD, deal type.

G1 Initial Qualification. Budget at least "exploring", start date within 6 months, Workday AE identified, deal type classified. Strategic logo override requires EVP approval.

Stage 2Intake & Triage

Owner: Deployment Strategy (SE) · AE supports

SE runs the qualification scorecard (see section 04 below), classifies the tier, and routes by deal type into Path A, B, or C.

G2 Pursuit Authorization by the Deployment Strategy Director. Eliminates the anti-pattern of AEs bypassing qualification by DM-ing SEs directly.

Stage 3Scoping

Owner: Deployment Strategy (SE) · Delivery as SME

SE runs discovery, completes the scope questionnaire, documents modules / integrations / conversions. Delivery SME involvement is formal-request-only with a context package. Gong AI captures the calls.

G3 Scope Lock. All sections complete, integrations classified, stakeholder alignment confirmed.

Stage 4Estimation

Owner: Deployment Strategy (SE)

Built in Project EDGE CPQ. Standard cost model (no geographic arbitrage), blended rate, contingency %. Output is the ROM and the priced scope.

G4 Estimation Quality. Uplifts documented, standard cost model used, margin in range. Deals >$700K require Delivery review.

Stage 5Deal Review

Owner: AE leads · Deployment Strategy / Delivery review

Lightweight (deals <$700K, standard scope): async approval via Teams, 24–48 hour turnaround, single approver. Formal (deals >$700K, non-standard, high-risk, or flagged): designated Delivery shepherd, SME validation of hours, Delivery Leadership sign-off, 3–5 business day turnaround.

G5 Commercial Approval. Delivery sign-off, EVP review for >$2M or strategic, margin meets minimum, resource availability confirmed via staffing ticket.

Stage 6Proposal / SOW

Owner: AE leads · Deployment Strategy generates

SE generates the proposal via Project EDGE: ROM summary, scope tables, integration schedule, order form, executive summary. SharePoint content library supplies reusable collateral.

Stage 7Negotiation

Owner: Sales (AE)

AE leads negotiation. Scope changes trigger a re-estimation loop back to Stage 4 (minutes via Project EDGE rather than days). Discount governance has approval thresholds.

Stage 8Handoff to Delivery

Owner: Delivery leads · Deployment Strategy generates the package

SE generates the S2S transition package: client context, scope decisions, risk register, assumptions, Gong recordings, resource plan. Formal S2S meeting plus 30-day transition support.

03.Three Paths Through the Process

Stage 2 routes every deal into one of three flows. Most net-new work follows Path A. Path C is decline-by-default.

Path A

Phase 1 / Phase X Deployment

Platform, HCM, HCM/PAY, FINS. Net new. Full 8-stage process. $700K–$5M+. This is the default Launch-shaped flow.

Path B

RFP Response

Adds a Bid/No-Bid gate and an RFP sub-process between Stage 2 and Stage 3. Same downstream gates apply.

Path C

Small Phase X / Single-SKU Add-On

Net-new Kainos client only. Abbreviated flow with a 0.7× qualification multiplier. Default: Decline unless strategic logo, platform expansion, relationship investment, or high margin.
