A.Start Here

The Pre-Sales repository on SharePoint is the single source of truth for how Kainos Americas pursues Workday opportunities. This page summarises what's in it. The actual files live in SharePoint — once you have access, the folder names here match one-to-one.

How to read this guide. Every section maps to a numbered top-level folder. Each card inside a section tells you what the asset is, when you'd reach for it, and whether it's Client Safe or Internal Only.

How we talk about ourselves

Kainos voice is honest, humble, familial, ambitious, confident. We say "partnership" not "engagement", "accelerator" not "tool". We avoid synergy, leverage-as-a-verb, best-in-class, world-class. We reference the client by name. Active voice — "Kainos delivers", not "is delivered by Kainos".

Two rules you cannot break

Pricing Guardrail

Investment, not cost

No hourly rates, no role-by-role hour breakdowns, no DB field names in any client-facing document. Phase totals must sum to overall. Round client-facing totals to nearest $5,000.

Client Data

Never cross-pollinate

One client's data never appears in another client's deliverable. Comparable deals are anonymised — "a similar healthcare implementation", never the name. Case studies are the only pre-approved public references.

B.Your First 30 Days

A suggested path. Adjust with your manager — but in this order you'll build context before you're asked to produce anything client-facing.

  1. Week 1 — Read the methodology. Start in 01_Methodology and Playbooks. Read the Launch methodology language doc and the six Non-Standard Implementation components. This is how we think about deals.
  2. Week 1 — Shadow two discovery calls. Ask your manager which active pursuits are at discovery stage and listen in. Read the Discovery Questionnaire in 03_Estimation/1-Discovery before the call.
  3. Week 2 — Walk the estimation models. Open the Workday Functional Uplifts Catalog, the Integrations Catalog, and the AC Prism Estimation Framework in 03_Estimation. Understand the inputs that drive a ROM.
  4. Week 2 — Read three case studies. Pick three from 04_RFP/Case Studies that match industries you'll likely cover. Notice how outcomes are written.
  5. Week 3 — Read the SOPs. 05_Enablement & Training has both the traditional and AI-integrated Pre-Sales SOPs plus three deep-dives (ROM, Integration Scoping, Scope Doc Generation).
  6. Week 3 — Sit in on a deal review. See how qualification, pricing, and margin land before anything goes to a client. Reference: Qualification Framework in 08_Sales Process and SOPs.
  7. Week 4 — Run a ROM yourself. Pick a closed-lost or sandbox deal with your manager and try the estimation workflow end-to-end. Get it reviewed before assuming it's right.

Non-Standard Implementations

Six components covering deals where a client already has a live Workday tenant. Every non-net-new opportunity routes through this framework before estimation begins.

ComponentWhat it does
1 — Intake & RoutingDecision tree that classifies a non-standard opportunity into one of five patterns (Consolidation, Divestiture, Global Expansion, Complex Module Add-On, Contained Module Add-On).
2 — Estimation ModelHow we size each pattern. Inputs, ranges, and the math behind the ROM.
3 — Scope Language LibraryReusable scope statements per pattern — drop into proposals and SOWs.
4 — Deal Review ChecklistThe gate before any non-standard deal goes to a client. Use it before commercial review.
5 — S2S Handoff PackageWhat Sales hands to Service at deal close so delivery starts with full context.
6 — EY Channel Operating ModelHow deals sourced through the EY partnership flow through pre-sales.

C.Glossary

Acronyms and shorthand that come up in every conversation.

Launch
Kainos's productised methodology for net-new Workday deployments. "Launch Flex" and "Launch Express" are sizing variants.
WDYW
Workday Done Your Way — non-Launch, custom-shaped engagements (typically for clients with an existing live tenant).
ROM
Rough Order of Magnitude estimate. The first defensible sizing of a deal.
S2S
Sales to Service. The handoff package and ceremony where a closed deal transitions from pre-sales to delivery.
FDM
Financial Data Model — the foundational data structure in Workday Financials.
ACC
Accounting Center.
Prism / AC
Workday Prism Analytics / Accounting Center — analytics and finance-extension capability areas with their own estimation framework.
OCM
Organisational Change Management.
LE / LEX
Launch Express templates used for integration scoping.
EDGE
Internal Kainos platform for pricing, estimation, and proposal automation.
KDT
Kainos Data Toolkit.
kbids
In-flight bid working files / folder.
